# ⚡ Bulk Emailer by Badal (Badal Mailer Pro)

> **The zero-subscription, local HTML mail merge Chrome Extension for Gmail.**  
> Send customized, beautifully formatted mass emails directly from your own Gmail inbox with live recipient previews, CSV file upload, and intelligent anti-spam delay throttling.

## 🌟 Why Badal Mailer Pro?

- **100% Free & Unlimited**: No 50-email daily caps, no hidden fees, no subscriptions.
- **Privacy First**: All CSV records and templates stay inside your browser (`mail.google.com`). No external servers.
- **In-Tab Floating Console**: Never loses your work if you click outside. The sleek modal stays open right inside Gmail.
- **Dynamic Variable Tags**: Insert `{{Name}}`, `{{Email}}`, `{{Institution}}`, `{{Research_Areas}}` anywhere in the subject or body.

- **Test Email Feature**: Send a 1-click test email to yourself first to verify mobile and desktop appearance.
- **Audit Logs & Export**: Live progress bar + downloadable CSV report of sent emails.

## 🚀 How to Install (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. In the address bar, type:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
   and press **Enter**.
3. In the top right corner, turn **ON** the toggle switch for **Developer mode**.
4. Click the button in the top left: **Load unpacked**.
5. Browse and select the folder:
   ```text
   C:\Users\User\bulk-emailer-by-badal
   ```
6. **Done!** You will see **Bulk Emailer by Badal** added to your Chrome extensions.

## ✉️ How to Use with Gmail

1. Open [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
2. Look at the bottom-right corner of your Gmail screen—you will see a stylish blue floating badge:
   ```text
   ⚡ Badal Mailer Pro
   ```
3. Click it to open the console:
   - **Step 1 (Recipients)**: Drag & drop your `recipients.csv` (or use the included `sample_recipients.csv`), or simply paste `Name, Email` lines.
   - **Step 2 (Template)**: Choose the preloaded **NSTC NanoSchool Mentor** template, or edit the HTML / Subject line.
   - **Step 3 (Preview)**: Click through the recipients (e.g. *Recipient 1 of 59*, *2 of 59*) to see the personalized email render live.
   - **Step 4 (Dispatch)**: 
     - Enter your personal email and click **Send Test** to inspect the real email in your inbox.
     - Choose **Auto-Send** or **Save to Drafts**.
     - Click **🚀 Start Mail Merge**!
